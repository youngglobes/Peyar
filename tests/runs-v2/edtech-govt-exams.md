# Naming: online coaching app for government-job exams (Hindi, tier-2/3 North and Central India)

## Assumptions
- Product: mobile-first coaching app (video classes, mock tests, daily practice) for SSC, banking and state PSC exams. Hindi first, then regional languages.
- Buyer: aspirants (and their families) in tier-2/3 towns of UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Haryana, Delhi-NCR. Price ₹99-499 a month, so the price is a mass-market, family-budget price.
- Tone: trustworthy, aspirational, friendly. Hindi or Hinglish welcome. Must work as an app-store name and a home-screen label.
- Scale: India only for the next 5 years. A .com is nice to have, not required. No founder name to use.
- Numerology not applied. Brand type and Nice classes: 41 (education), 9 (app), 16 (books/printed material).

## Naming brief
**Brand type:** credence (a family cannot judge the coaching until the exam result), sold like search (the aspirant compares apps in the Play Store). The name must feel trustworthy and be easy to type and say.
**Category norm (scan of live competitors):** Testbook, Adda247, Careerwill, Mahendras, Oliveboard, Utkarsh, Drishti, Prepp, Kadmik, Tayari24, Taiyari Karlo, Dabra Classes, SATHEE (IIT Kanpur, free), PW's SSC batches. The pattern is a founder or institute name (Mahendras, Utkarsh, Dabra), an English "test/prep" word with a number or "book" (Testbook, Adda247, Prepp), or a Hindi aspiration or readiness word (Tayari, Kadmik, Careerwill). Names are mostly short, 2-3 syllables, and nearly all are Latin-script. Sources: [Testbook listing](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/testbook/id1666802218), [Adda247](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/adda247/id1618943840), [Careerwill](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1584116612), [Kadmik](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/kadmik/id1576656198), [Tayari24](https://yourstory.com/companies/tayari24).
**Decision: inside the norm, with a single warm Hindi word.** The audience buys on trust and familiarity, not novelty. A contrarian coined name would need a budget to teach it. A Hindi word that names the emotion of the aspirant is instantly legible in Hindi-belt towns and still ownable, because most Hindi aspiration words (Lakshya, Manzil, Safar, Jeet, Pragati, Siddhi, Uday) are already taken in this category.
**Feel:** open vowels, two syllables, up to 7 letters, a word a mother can say to her neighbour. No abstract claim words. No place names. No "University", "Institute" or "Academy" in the legal name.

## Founder's name: Bharat Digital University
**Verdict: do not use it. It fails on the "University" word (UGC Act s.23), it is generic, and at 10 syllables it will not survive a phone screen or a home screen.**

Checks run on the name, as for any candidate:
- **Sound / spelling (score.py): 47/100.** 10 syllables, 23 letters. A home-screen label cuts at about 12 characters ("Bharat Digit..."). On the phone it will be said "BDU" or "Bharat wali university".
- **Meaning screen:** Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali: no slang or negative reading found, high confidence. The issue is not slang but a claim: "University" tells a family that the app gives degrees. It does not, and does not need to.
- **Legal, UGC Act s.23 (D6):** only a university established under a Central, Provincial or State Act may use the word "University", and the UGC has acted on this, for example asking 123 deemed universities to drop it and warning students about the "Digital University of Skill Resurgence" ([Times Higher Education](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/indian-institutions-barred-using-university-title), [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/ugc-cautions-students-against-taking-admission-in-digital-university-of-skill-resurgence/amp), [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/857640/scroll_in)). A coaching app has no such status. This is a D-group FAIL under the process.
- **Legal, descriptive (D3):** "Digital University" is the generic description of an online university. It cannot be monopolised as a mark and is very crowded: there is a government-backed National Digital University, the NIELIT Digital University, and a Kerala digital university ([Testbook explainer](https://testbook.com/amp/ias-preparation/national-digital-university), [Digital India](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/press_release/union-minister-shri-ashwini-vaishnaw-launches-the-nielit-digital-university-a-platform-designed-to-democratize-access-to-high-quality-digital-education/)). Another D-group FAIL.
- **Existing use (search):** no business under the exact name "Bharat Digital University" turned up, which is weak evidence of a free name. The search returned government "Digital University" projects, so the name reads as a government institution and will confuse buyers and regulators. The .com, .in and .co.in all showed as free, and so did the YouTube and X handles, which is the one good sign.
- **MCA (D4, UNVERIFIED):** "Bharat" can read as a link to the nation or government (Rule 8A and 8B territory); a university-like name is also easy to flag for objection. It must be checked live on the MCA site.
- **Fit with the brief:** a "University" name promises degrees the app cannot give, and it is formal and distant, not friendly. The audience would hear a degree factory, not a friend who keeps your hausla up. It is long for an app-store title and for SMS and UPI display (no workable 6-character short form beyond "BDU").
- **36-point score (score36.py): 55 (5 unverified), OUT (D-group FAIL: D3, D6).** Other fails: B1 (10 syllables), B6 (confusable with National Digital University), C6 (the name is a claim), F2 (23 letters).

**What to keep from the founder's instinct:** "Bharat" is a good feeling for this audience (Bharat, not India). Keep it as a descriptor line instead of in the legal name, for example "Hausla: Bharat ka sarkari naukri coach" (to be re-checked against MCA and trade mark rules before it goes on any board). If the founder wants a longer, institutional-sounding name, the closest safe forms are a calm brand word plus a plain descriptor such as "Hausla Exam Prep", never "University".

## Next steps
1. Native-speaker check: top 3 names (Hausla, Shikhar, Kadam) to 1 person per target language: Hindi (UP/Bihar), Rajasthani/Haryanvi, Marathi, and one each for Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam if those regional languages are part of the launch.

3. IP India search in classes 41 and 9 (and 16 for books) for each of the top 3: wordmark "match", "contains" and phonetic. Then a trade mark agent's opinion, then file TM-A.
4. Reserve the company name on MCA (SPICe+ Part A / RUN). Do not use "University", "Institute" or "Academy" in the legal name.
5. Buy domains and claim handles the same day. Because the exact .com of every top name is held, plan on a modifier (get-, -app, -prep) and register the .in. Open the taken X and YouTube handles by hand to see what they are.
Expect weeks, not days. Unhype took a studio about 3 months (strategy, shortlist, market test, trade mark, multi-geography, stakeholder sign-off).

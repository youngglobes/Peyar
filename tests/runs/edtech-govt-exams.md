# Peyar test run: govt-exam coaching app (edtech)

Date: 2026-11-20. Brief: online coaching app for government-job exams (SSC, banking, state PSC) in Hindi and regional languages, tier-2/3 North and Central India, Rs 99-499/month, tone trustworthy / aspirational / friendly, Hindi or Hinglish welcome, app-store-friendly. Founder already likes "Bharat Digital University".

## Assumptions
- Full brief given, so no questions asked.
- Launch in Hindi belt (UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Haryana, Delhi); 5-year reach is pan-India, still Hindi-first. Export (F4) is N-A.
- Domain is a nice-to-have, not a hard constraint ("app-store-friendly" read as: short, easy to type and spell, searchable in Hindi and Roman letters). So the step-4.5 domain filter was skipped.
- Nice classes: 41 (education), 9 (app software), 42 (SaaS). Founder name format not wanted.
- Numerology not mentioned.

## Naming brief
Brand type: **credence** (the buyer cannot judge teaching quality until the result arrives) with a **search** layer (people find apps by typing a name into Play Store). So: trust first, easy to type second.
Category norm (web search + playbook 9): the leaders use founder/teacher names or Hinglish or plain English tags (Testbook, Adda247, Oliveboard, Prepp, Examsbook, Smartkeeda, Mahendras, Utkarsh, Careerwill, Kadmik, Physics Wallah, Tayari24; sources: Play Store / App Store listings returned by the search). Norm in one sentence: **English-descriptive "test/exam/prep" names or a teacher's name, with a minority of Hindi aspiration words.**
Decision: **mostly contrarian-but-warm.** Play with Hindi everyday words (not "test/prep"), because every incumbent sounds the same and tier-2/3 users speak Hindi. Feel: hopeful, local, one step from the family. Sound: two syllables, open vowels, easy to type in Roman letters. No-go: "University/Institute/Academy" (UGC rule), "Guaranteed/No.1" claims, "Sarkari" as the core word (generic), deity or caste words.

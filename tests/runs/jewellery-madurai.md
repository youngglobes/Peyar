# Peyar test run: Madurai lightweight gold and diamond jewellery

## Assumptions
- Brand: lightweight daily-wear gold + diamond jewellery, mid-premium; 2 stores in Madurai plus online; pan-India within 5 years. Buyers: working women 25-40.
- Tone: modern, trustworthy, warm. Tamil or Sanskrit welcome. No hard domain constraint (a .com is nice, not required), so step 4.5 (early domain gate) was skipped; domains were checked at step 5.
- Founder name okay? Not stated; I did not use a founder name. Numerology: not mentioned, ignored.
- TM classes: 14 (jewellery), 35 (retail / online store).
- Founder's own name "National Gold House" is evaluated in its own section below, as the skill requires.
- Web search was available and used, but the search index is weak for small Indian brands. "No result found" is NOT evidence that a name is free.

## Naming brief
- Brand type: credence (purity, trust) sold like search online (buyers compare weights and prices).
- Category norm (scan, 8-10 names): incumbents are heritage/family/"Sri ... Jewellers" or Sanskrit-premium (Tanishq, Kalyan, Malabar, GRT, Joyalukkas, Bhima, Senco; Madurai locals Thangamayil, Sumathi, Siva Jewellers, Srikrishna Nagai Maligai). The modern online and lightweight wave uses short English or invented words (CaratLane, Melorra, GIVA, BlueStone, PNG LiteStyle, Candere, which also has a Madurai store). Norm in one sentence: older Indian jewellery = family/heritage/"Gold" words, newer lightweight/online jewellery = short, soft, brandable single words. Sources: [Madurai jewellers search](https://www.thangamayil.com/corporate/?p=30732), [Melorra](https://melorra.com/jewellery/nose-pins/), [Candere Madurai](https://store.candere.com/city/madurai/hyper-page/south-indian-jewellery), [GIVA](https://www.giva.co/blogs/tales/the-best-gold-and-diamond-jewellery-for-working-women-comfort-and-style).
- Decision: play inside the "short soft single word" modern lane, not the heritage "Gold House / X Jewellers" lane, because the buyer is a 25-40 working woman and the product is lightweight. Warmth from a Tamil word; no claim words; no "Gold" in the name (boxes out diamonds, and is descriptive).
- Feel: soft open vowels, 2-3 syllables, <=7 letters, easy for a Delhi or Dubai customer to say. No-go: "Gold", "Diamond", "Jewel", "National", "Premium", deity names.

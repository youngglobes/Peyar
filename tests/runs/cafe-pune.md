# Peyar test run: Pune chai and snacks cafe chain

## Assumptions
- Brief was complete, so no questions asked. Assumed: chai + snacks (vada pav, bun maska, samosa, maggi-style), counter and sit-down cafe, mass-to-mid price (cutting chai Rs 15-20, full chai Rs 30-60).
- Footprint: 1 outlet in Pune now, 10 in Maharashtra in 3 years (Pune, Mumbai, Nashik, Nagpur, Kolhapur), franchise possible later. So the name must work for Marathi AND Hindi speakers and not be city-bound.
- Audience: college students and young office goers. Tone playful, desi, energetic. Marathi or Hindi welcome, avoid English-only.
- Constraints not stated, so assumed: .com/.in are nice-to-have, not hard requirements; founder name not wanted; numerology not applied. Trade mark classes: 43 (cafe service), 30 (packaged chai, snacks), 35 (franchising).
- Gandhi Chai Adda was supplied by the founder and is evaluated in its own section.

## Naming brief
**Brand type:** experience (cafe), with a search element for packaged chai later. The name must be easy to recommend ("chal, X pe milte") and say-able once to a friend.
**Category norm:** web search of chai-cafe players found: Chaayos (pun on chai + "chaos/chai-os"), Chai Point (plain English), Chai Sutta Bar (Hindi slang), Chai Garam (Hindi), Chaiwala-style "Chai Thela", "Chai Nagri", "ChaiSutra", "Tea Trails", "Chai Pe Chuski", "Chai Katta" (Bavdhan, Pune), "The Chai Chaska Bar & Cafe" (Pune), Chaikund (Pune). Norm = the word "Chai" plus a Hindi or English pun or place word; "Chai X" is crowded and mostly descriptive. Sources: [Chaayos/QZ](https://qz.com/999604/chaayos-a-cafe-chain-that-is-giving-indians-exactly-what-they-want-the-perfect-cup-of-chai), [Chai Point](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Chai_Point), [Pune cafe listings](https://lbb.in/pune/tea-rooms-in-pune), [Chai Pe Chuski](https://www.franchiseindia.com/brands/chai-pe-chuski.112854). Store counts in those articles are dated; not re-verified.
**Position: slightly contrarian.** Drop the word "chai" as a prefix and use one distinctive Marathi/Hindi word or coinage, so the name is ownable and can later carry snacks, packaged chai and franchise outlets. The sound should be short, open and playful.
**No-go zones:** deity/leader names (Shivaji, Gandhi, Ganesh), generic "Chai + X", words already used by Pune chai cafes (Katta, Chaska, Chuski), words with slang risk in Hindi/Marathi.

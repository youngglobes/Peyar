# Naming: fresh-cooked pet food subscription (Mumbai, pan-India metros)

## Assumptions
- Sells vet-formulated, fresh-cooked dog and cat meals on a monthly subscription, delivered chilled. Based in Mumbai, shipping to metro cities within 5 years. Premium tier, ₹3,000-6,000 a month.
- Buyers are urban pet parents aged 25-40: English-first, social-media native, comfortable with Hindi/Hinglish. They treat the pet as family and read ingredient labels.
- Name reach: **mix** (default), so at least half the pool is global-neutral and at most half India-rooted. No languages to include or avoid were given.
- A .com is nice, not required. Founder name is not used (none given). Numerology is not a constraint.
- Nice classes to search later: 31 (pet food), 29 (prepared meals), 35 (subscription retail/D2C), 44 (vet services, if added).

## Naming brief
**Brand type:** search (buyers compare online, read reviews, Google it) with strong credence elements (a vet-formulated diet for a family member; the buyer cannot judge nutrition after tasting). The name must be easy to find and spell, and it must feel safe and warm rather than gimmicky.

**Category norm (scan of live competitors):** Doggie Dabbas, Fresh Fetch, Petura, Benny's Bowl, Pet Gourmet, BLEP, Sara's Wholesome Food (Heads Up For Tails), Drools Fresh, Canine India, Gourmutt, Supertails and Heads Up For Tails. India's fresh-pet-food names are mostly **playful English food-plus-pet words** ("Bowl", "Fresh", "Dabba", "Paws", "Tails", "Fetch"), plus a few Hinglish "dabba" names. "Bowl", "Fresh" and "Tail(s)" are crowded.

**Decision:** inside the norm on tone (warm, playful, a pet word or a kitchen word) but **outside it on the vocabulary**. Avoid "Fresh", "Bowl", "Dabba" and "Fetch". Aim for one kitchen image the buyer can picture, with a trustworthy sound.

**Naming brief:** idea = a home kitchen that cooks for your pet, run by vets. Feel = warm, a little playful, never medical-cold. Sound = 2-3 syllables, open and easy over the phone. No-go zones: "Fresh", "Bowl", "Dabba", "Pure/Natural" (FSSAI-claim words), cure/heal/health claims, and anything that implies spicy food for pets (tadka, masala).

## Candidates (by method)
**Real word, repurposed:** Simmer (slow-cooked care) · Ladle (the cooking spoon) · Hearth (home kitchen warmth) · Larder (a well-stocked pantry) · Gravy (the comfort of a meal)
**Compound:** Tailpot (tail plus cooking pot) · Bowlwise (smart feeding) · Wagpot (happy pot) · Purrpot (cat-friendly pot) · Stockwag (stock plus wag)
**Coined / invented:** Nomi (soft, friendly) · Zuppa (Italian "soup") · Fetchly (fetch plus -ly) · Tolo (short, easy) · Yumo (yum plus o) · Pawra (paw plus aura) · Dolo (short, open vowels)
**Alphanumeric:** Bowl400 (Mumbai pin prefix 400) · Tail91 (tail plus India dial code +91) · Chill4 (served chilled at 4 degrees C) · Paws91 (paws plus +91)
**Regional / Hinglish (India-rooted):** Katori (Hindi/Marathi for small bowl) · Chulha (Hindi stove) · Tadka (Hindi tempering) · Sappadu (Tamil "meal") · Aahaar (Sanskrit/Hindi "food")
**Evocative:** Warm Bowl (cozy meal) · Ember (low, steady heat) · Sunday Pot (Sunday home cooking) · Second Helping (generosity)
**Contrarian / anti-category:** Unkibble (rejects kibble)
**Place-based:** Bandra Bowl (Mumbai neighbourhood) · Colaba Pot (Mumbai neighbourhood)

That is 33 candidates across 9 methods. Global-neutral methods make up 24 of 33 (73%); India-rooted are 5 (15%); place-based 2 and contrarian 1 sit outside both lists. Alphanumeric: 4.

### 2. Descriptive / generic (D3)
- **Aahaar** and **Sappadu**: the plain Hindi/Sanskrit and Tamil words for "food" and "meal". Cut as descriptive and unprotectable.
- **Warm Bowl** and **Bowlwise**: "bowl" is a crowded category word (Benny's Bowl, BLEP). Cut.
- **Gravy**: a standard wet-food term; search shows Drools, Heads Up For Tails (Hearty, Sara's), Purepet, Kennel Kitchen and Purple Tail all sell "gravy" products. Cut as descriptive and crowded.

### 3. Existing use (web search + domain-holder lines; searches found no pet brand under any survivor name, which is weak evidence in India)
- **Unkibble**: unkibble.com redirects to spotandtango.com (US fresh dog food brand). Same category. Cut.
- **Fetchly**: too close to Fresh Fetch (live Bangalore fresh-meal competitor in the category scan). Cut.
- **Hearth**: a live brand "Hearty" sells dog food in India (Heads Up For Tails); Hearth/Hearty is a near clash; hearth.in is parked. Cut.
- **Bowl400 / Bandra Bowl**: "Bowl" echoes Benny's Bowl, a same-category fresh-food brand; Bowl400 also reads like a 400 ml bowl or 400 g pack. Cut.
- **Wagpot, Purrpot**: "Wag!" (US) and the "-pot" cat-only reading box the brand in and clash. Cut (also fails the one-brand-for-dog-and-cat need).
- **Stockwag, Pawra, Colaba Pot**: no evidence of use, but ugly or boxed-in: see fit gate. Cut.
- **Paws91**: Paws India (Mumbai, IndiaMART listing for dog biscuits) and PawsIndia (pet e-commerce) are same-category users of "Paws India". Cut.
- **Chill4**: no search run; cut at fit because it reads "chill for" in English and gives no pet or food cue.
- **Second Helping**: not run through search; cut at fit because it implies greed or leftovers.

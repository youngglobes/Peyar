# Naming: fast-charging network for electric 2W/3W (Hyderabad to pan-India)

## Assumptions
- Name reach: **mix** (default). At least half the pool is global-neutral because SEA is planned.
- Brand will be a consumer app plus B2B fleet product, so the name must work in an app-store listing and on a fleet-ops contract.
- A .com **or** .in is required (either is enough); .ai is nice to have.
- Founder name and numerology not required. Price tier: low per-session cost for riders, volume contracts for fleets.
- Categories (Nice): 9 (app), 39 (charging/mobility services), 35 (B2B fleet services), 42 (platform). IP India and MCA are not searched live.

## Naming brief
**Brand type:** search + experience. Riders compare apps and chargers, and quality (uptime) is felt only after use, so the name must be easy to find, spell, and say once to a friend.
**Category norm (scan):** Statiq, ChargeZone, Zeon, Bolt.Earth, Tata Power EZ Charge, Mahindra CHARGE_iN, GLIDA, eVolt, EV Plugs, PlugNGo, Hopcharge, ThunderPlus/Bijliride. The norm is short coined or compound English with a charge/volt/plug/bolt signal (Statiq, ChargeZone, eVolt, Bolt.Earth), plus corporate parent names (Tata, Mahindra). Regional-language names are almost absent.
**Decision:** inside the norm on sound (short, techy, English-travelling) but contrarian on structure: a name that avoids the crowded "Charge/Plug/Bolt/EV" words, so it is ownable in a field full of near-identical names. Alphanumeric options give the 2W/3W or Hyderabad story directly.
**Feel:** reliable, techy, quick. Hard stops (k, t, p, v), 2-3 syllables, travels to Singapore, Jakarta and Manila without an Indian context. No-go: speed claims ("10min"), "Charge/Plug/Bolt/EV" as the core word, deity names, city names.

## Candidates (by method)
**Real word, repurposed:** Relay (hand-off, always on) · Tempo (pace; also Indian 3W term) · Jolt (quick energy) · Surge (power rush) · Spark (ignition, start)
**Compound:** Voltmate (rider's companion) · Ampway (route to power) · Wattway (path of watts) · Plugstop (pit-stop for plugs) · Gridhop (hop between grid points) · Zipvolt (fast volt)
**Coined:** Zivo · Voltra · Chargo · Rivolt · Amperra · Zaptu (zap + quick) · Kilovo (kilo + volt) · Wattio
**Alphanumeric:** Amp23 (2- and 3-wheelers) · Watt23 (2W + 3W) · Amp91 (+91 India) · Volt78 (78 E, Hyderabad's meridian) · Charge23
**Evocative:** Topup · Brisk · Zing
**Sanskrit:** Vidyut (electricity) · Tvara (haste) · Vega (speed) · Urja (energy)
**Hinglish:** Plugwala · Chaarji · Voltwala

Pool: 33 names across 8 methods. Global-neutral 26 (79%), India-rooted 7 (21%), alphanumeric 5.

## Survivors
Cut order: meaning, descriptive (D3), existing use, sound (<60), domains (hard constraint: .com or .in free). Sound scores were all above 60.

**Cuts before the table:**
- **D3 descriptive/generic:** Vidyut and Urja are the plain Sanskrit words for electricity/energy (descriptive, crowded, "vidyut" is also a common firm prefix); Topup is the generic word for recharge; Spark, Surge, Jolt, Relay, Tempo, Brisk, Zing are generic single words and every one has .com and .in both taken (script).
- **Existing use:** Rivolt: near-identical to Revolt Motors (EV two-wheelers, same audience); Plugstop: echoes PlugNGo and EV Plugs (search results, same category); Gridhop: echoes Hopcharge (search result, same category); Voltwala: echoes eVolt India (search result, same category).
- **Domain (hard constraint, `check_name.py` shows both .com and .in taken):** Wattway, Zivo, Voltra, Chargo, Amperra, Charge23, Tvara, Wattly, Ampiq, Revvolt, Voltaro, Plugzy, Ampzo, Voltiq, Vyro, Ampway, Vega, Urja. Chargly, Joltly and Wattio have .in free but were dropped on taste (Chargly/Joltly: "-ly" suffix feels dated per the SaaS playbook; Wattio: weak sound/meaning link) and were not given a search.

**Fit-gate rejections (survivors not taken to finalists):**
- Amp91: same crowded "Amp" family as Amp23, and "91" adds nothing for a charging network (Amp23 is the stronger of the pair).
- Watt23: "Watt" + number is generic-sounding, 4 letters of ambiguity with "Watts"/"Wattz" spellings; weaker than Amp23.
- Zipvolt: "Zip" speed claim and hard to dictate on a phone ("zip-volt" vs "zipp-vault"); .com taken.
- Voltmate: generic compound, no ownable core, .com taken.
- Chaarji: Hinglish does not travel to SEA and feels consumer-casual for B2B fleet buyers (contradicts "techy").

## Next steps
1. Native-speaker check: top 3 names to 1 person per target language (Telugu, Hindi, Urdu, plus a Bahasa or Tagalog speaker for SEA).
2. 5-second recall test with 10-20 target buyers (riders and fleet managers) (script below).
3. IP India search in classes 9, 39, 35, 42 (wordmark + phonetic; especially "KILO", "AMP", "VOLT") → TM agent's opinion → file TM-A.
4. Reserve the company name on MCA (SPICe+ Part A / RUN).
5. Buy domains and claim handles the same day (for Kilovo, approach the .com holder; also take the Play Store and App Store name).
Expect weeks, not days. Unhype took a studio ~3 months (strategy → shortlist → market test → TM → multi-geography → stakeholder sign-off).

**Recall test script:** show each name for 5 seconds, talk about something else for 2 minutes, then ask: "Which names do you remember? Spell one. What do you think it sells?" Recall + correct spelling + right category guess wins.

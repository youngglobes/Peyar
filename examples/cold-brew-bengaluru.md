# Brand name options: Bengaluru ready-to-drink cold brew

## Assumptions
- Product is a bottled and canned ready-to-drink (RTD) cold brew, 200-250 ml, sold in quick-commerce apps (Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart), cafes and later modern trade. Likely trade mark classes: **30** (coffee, coffee-based beverages), **32** (non-alcoholic beverages), **35** (retail/D2C), **43** (if you add cafes).
- Name reach = **mix** (default). It has to work in Bengaluru and Delhi first, then be said without explanation in Singapore and Dubai within 3 years. A .com matters for the export phase, but is treated as "nice, not required".
- Tone: bold, energetic, modern. Audience 20-35 urban professionals and students, who find products on a quick-commerce app grid, so the name must be legible as a tiny thumbnail and typeable into a search bar.
- Founder name and numerology: no constraint given. No languages to avoid or require.
- Web search was available and used. IP India, MCA and Instagram were **not** checked live, so they are UNVERIFIED throughout.

## Naming brief
**Brand type:** search (the buyer compares cans on an app grid before tasting), with experience elements (taste is only known after the first can). The name carries the heaviest load of the three types: it must be findable, spellable and recallable in one glance.

**Category-norm scan (web search, plus playbook for beverage norms):**
| Brand | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Sleepy Owl (Delhi; bottles, packs, cans) | Playful animal-plus-habit English |
| Blue Tokai (cans, cafes) | Place or origin name (a Tokai is a coffee estate in Coorg) plus colour |
| Rage Coffee (RTD cans, 240 ml, about ₹140 in a 2023 report) | Single emotive real word |
| ITC Sunbean (cans, July 2026 launch) | Corporate sub-brand, compound |
| Tata Coffee Gold / Tata Coffee Grand | Parent-brand extension |
| Nescafe Intense Cafe | Parent-brand extension, descriptive |
| Bolt (Hyderabad, bottled cold brew) | Single speed word |
| Araku, Koffelo (NOC), Macobrew, DropKaffe | Origin name, coined K-spelling, compound |
| Paper Boat (playbook example, beverages) | Evocative nostalgia |

**Norm in one sentence:** Indian RTD coffee is short English words, mostly emotive or playful (Rage, Bolt, Sleepy Owl), or a coffee-roaster origin name (Blue Tokai, Araku), with parent-brand extensions from the big groups.

**Decision: inside the norm on form, contrarian on the empty spot.** The crowded lane is the playful or sleepy one and the origin-heritage one. The energetic, high-speed lane is held only by Rage and Bolt. A short, hard-consonant energetic name sits inside the one-or-two-words format shoppers expect, while avoiding "Owl", "Brew House", "Tokai" and plain "Rage"/"Bolt" clones.

**Naming brief:**
- Idea: coffee that is cold, fast and charged. Bengaluru energy without a Bengaluru-only name.
- Feel: bold, modern, urban. Short, one or two syllables if possible, hard stops (k, t, j, d) for energy, one clean vowel.
- Sound: has to survive v/w and z/j merging across accents, and be typeable on a phone keypad without spelling help.
- No-go zones: "Kaapi" or "Brew" as the core (descriptive, crowded), abstract claim words (premium, best, pure), speed names too close to Bolt or Rage, alcohol-sounding numerics (Bira 91 echo), deity or caste words, "Cold Brew 18 hours" style claims that are already Blue Tokai and Steep 18 copy.

**Fit-gate rejections (stage 5):**
- 77E: "seventy-seven E" is awkward to say aloud and `77e.in` is held by someone; nothing in the name says coffee or energy.
- Zyra: Zehra echo is risky for a drink, and `.com` is live; weaker than the other coined option.
- Tavo: both `.com` and `.in` live and the sound is soft, not bold.
- Ojas: wellness/Ayurveda cliche feel, soft, and `.com` and `.in` both live; does not match "bold, energetic, modern".
- Peakcup: "cup" mismatches a can and bottle format; Peak RTD coffee exists in the US.

Finalists (5): Jetbrew, Coldfuse, 560 Cold, Vega, Kovo. Spread: 5 methods (compound x2, alphanumeric, Sanskrit, coined). Global-neutral count: 4 of 5. Alphanumeric included.

## Naming brief
**Brand type:** search (shelf, marketplace, Instagram) plus experience (taste). The name must be findable, spellable and easy to recommend. Credence cues (claims) belong on the pack, not in the name.

**Category norm (scan, 2026-11-20):** Millet snack brands are mostly English-coined or product-led names with a wellness cue, and the Tamil Nadu D2C names that stand out lean on heritage or a word for taste. Examples seen: Tata Soulfull, Slurrp Farm, Kiru (Millet Crunchies), Troo Good, WellBe Foods, Sweet Karam Coffee, GudFudBox, Marutham Agencies (B2B). Other known names: Early Foods, Wonder Grains, Yoga Bar. Norm in one sentence: English or wellness-coded names (Soulfull, WellBe, Troo Good) with a few regional or playful outliers (Sweet Karam Coffee, GudFudBox).

**Recommendation: contrarian-regional.** A short Tamil word from the field, soil or sparrow world sits outside the English-wellness norm, fits "warm, modern, rooted", and avoids "healthy/diet" talk. Cost: it must travel beyond Tamil Nadu, so the pack needs a one-line story.

**Naming brief:** Idea = the small grain, the field, the sparrow that eats it. Feel = warm, light, a little playful. Sound = 2-3 syllables, open vowels, easy for a Hindi speaker to say once. No-go: grain names (Thinai, Varagu, Samai, Kambu are generic, s.9), claim words, anything that says "health" or "diet", city names in the core.

Sources for the scan: [YourStory on Tamil Nadu D2C brands](https://yourstory.com/2024/07/how-d2c-brands-from-tamilnadu-have-created-a-niche), [Inc42 on Sweet Karam Coffee](https://inc42.com/startups/how-sweet-karam-coffee-grew-to-%e2%82%b946-cr-by-turning-regional-snacks-into-daily-repeat/), [WellBe Foods launch](https://theprint.in/ani-press-releases/wellbe-foods-expands-its-healthy-snacking-portfolio-with-the-launch-of-millet-based-snacks/2833806).

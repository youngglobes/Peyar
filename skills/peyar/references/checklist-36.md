# The Peyar 36-point naming checklist

Score every finalist (top 4–5) on all 36 points. Each point gets **PASS / WARN / FAIL / N-A / UNVERIFIED** plus one line of evidence.

Source tags:
- `[V]`: idea raised by Harish Venkatesh (Become, formerly Frozen Iris) in the Nuke It podcast "80 Minutes to Understand Branding Like a Pro". His studio uses a 36-parameter process that he does not list in full. This file is **not** his list.
- `[R]`: India-specific research added by Peyar.

A name with **any FAIL in group D (Legal)** is out, however well it scores elsewhere.

---

## A. Strategy fit (6)

| # | Check | PASS | WARN | FAIL | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Brand type weight**: search, experience or credence (see `brand-types.md`) | Name carries the load that type needs (search: clarity/recall; credence: trust) | Neutral | Works against the type (for example, a jokey name for a cancer clinic) | V |
| A2 | **Category norm**: inside or a deliberate contrarian play | Choice is explicit and justified | Accidentally half-in, half-out | Copies the leader so closely that it reads as a knock-off | V |
| A3 | **Positioning match**: does the name say what the brand stands for? | Name echoes the one idea (for example, Unhype = no-nonsense skincare) | Neutral word, meaning must be built | Contradicts positioning | V |
| A4 | **Audience fit**: tier-1 English speakers, Bharat/regional, B2B buyers, Gen Z | Sounds native to that audience | Works for part of the audience | Alienates the core buyer | R |
| A5 | **Geography**: India-only vs global ambition | Works in every target geography | Needs a pronunciation hint abroad | Hard to say or negative in a planned market | V |
| A6 | **Room to grow**: not boxed into one product or city | Survives line extensions (Urban Company > UrbanClap) | Mild limit | Name dies when the second product launches (for example, "Chennai Idli Co" going pan-India with biryani) | R |

## B. Sound & memory (6)

| # | Check | PASS | WARN | FAIL | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Syllables**: fewer syllables means higher recall | 1–3 | 4 | 5+ | V |
| B2 | **Vowel feel**: open vowels feel light and premium; hard stops feel strong | Feel matches the intended tone | Neutral | Opposite of the intended tone | V |
| B3 | **Pan-Indian pronounceability**: survives v/w, z/j, f/ph, sh/s, th/t, short/long vowel swaps | One way to say it | Two acceptable ways | People will split into camps | R |
| B4 | **Phone-dictation test**: say it once on a call; can the listener spell it? | Yes | Needs one spelling hint | Needs letter-by-letter spelling | R |
| B5 | **Truecaller / contact-save test**: how will customers save and search it? | Saved as-is | Minor variants | Gets saved as something else ("Iris Harish", "Frizz") | V |
| B6 | **Homophones / confusables**: sounds like a competitor, a swear word or a generic word | None | Distant | Clear clash | R |

## C. Meaning & culture (6)

| # | Check | PASS | WARN | FAIL | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Multi-language meaning**: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu + English (see `indian-languages.md`) | Neutral or positive everywhere checked | Odd but harmless somewhere | Negative meaning in a target-market language | V |
| C2 | **Slang / offensive**: including sub-strings and how it reads when split | Clean | Joke potential | Vulgar or mockable | V |
| C3 | **Religious / caste / political / community sensitivity** | None | Uses a deity or sacred word (legal but risky for food, footwear, alcohol and similar) | Caste marker, slur or political symbol | R |
| C4 | **Positive connotation**: does it evoke something good? | Clear positive | Neutral | Negative or sad | V |
| C5 | **Known word vs invented**: an invented or foreign word must be taught (the Frozen Iris lesson); a known word arrives with meaning (Become) | Known word, or invented and the budget to teach it | Invented, modest budget | Invented, near-zero budget, search brand | V |
| C6 | **No abstract claim words**: avoid "quality", "world-class", "premium", "best", "No.1", "state-of-the-art" | None | One soft claim word | Name is a claim | V |

## D. Legal, India (6). Any FAIL = drop

| # | Check | PASS | WARN | FAIL | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Trade mark search**: IP India public search, wordmark + phonetic, in your Nice class(es) | No identical or similar mark in your class | Similar mark in a related class, or an abandoned or refused mark | Identical or confusingly similar live mark in your class | V |
| D2 | **Well-known marks** (TM Act s.11): near a famous mark in **any** class | Clear | Faint echo | Evokes a well-known mark (for example, "Tatafresh", "Amulya Dairy") | R |
| D3 | **Distinctiveness** (TM Act s.9): not merely descriptive or generic | Arbitrary, suggestive or coined | Suggestive but close to descriptive | Purely descriptive ("Best Dental Clinic", "Fresh Milk") | R |
| D4 | **MCA name rules**: Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014, Rule 8 (resemblance), 8A (undesirable), 8B (words needing govt approval, such as National, Union, Central, Board, Commission, Authority, Republic, Federal) | Clean | Needs a regulator NOC word (Bank, Insurance, Mutual Fund, Nidhi, Stock Exchange, Venture Capital) and you are regulated | Uses a restricted word without eligibility, or resembles an existing company | R |
| D5 | **Emblems & Names Act 1950**: names and emblems in its Schedule (UN, WHO, national flag, Ashoka emblem, govt seals, names or pictures of Mahatma Gandhi, Nehru, Shivaji, the PM, and others) | Clean | Uses a word that is **also a common surname** or ordinary word (Gandhi, Shivaji) without clearly invoking the leader: verify with an agent | Clearly invokes a scheduled person, emblem or body (portrait, "Mahatma", "Bapu", flag or emblem imagery, UN/WHO) | R |
| D6 | **Sector regulator words**: RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, NMC/DCI, AYUSH, FSSAI, UGC, Bar Council restrictions on names and claims | Clean | Claim-adjacent in a non-health category ("Guaranteed", "Doctor's choice") | Prohibited claim or ethics breach. **Always FAIL:** "Cure"/"Heal"/disease names for supplements, ayurveda, food or pharma (Drugs & Magic Remedies Act; UK/EU/GCC claim rules); "University" without UGC status; "Bank" without a licence | R |

## E. Digital availability (6)

| # | Check | PASS | WARN | FAIL | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **.com** | Exact match free | Free with a modifier, or held by an unrelated/parked/no-site holder (see ↳ line) | Held by a **same-category** active business | R |
| E2 | **.in / .co.in** | Exact match free | One of the two free, or holders unrelated/parked | Both held by a **same-category** active business | R |
| E3 | **Instagram handle** | Exact match | Modifier needed | Taken by a same-category brand | V |
| E4 | **YouTube channel / @handle** | Exact match | Modifier needed | Taken by a same-category channel | V |
| E5 | **X / LinkedIn page / WhatsApp Business display name** | Available | Modifier needed | Same-category conflict | V |
| E6 | **Search & app-store clash**: Google page 1, Play Store, Amazon/Flipkart brand filter | Owns page 1 eventually | Shares page 1 with an unrelated big entity | Same-category competitor dominates | R |

## F. Execution (6)

| # | Check | PASS | WARN | FAIL | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Script rendering**: written in Devanagari, Tamil and other target scripts, does it look and read right? | Clean in every script | Awkward conjunct | Reads as a different or bad word | R |
| F2 | **Wordmark friendliness**: length, letter shapes, ascenders and descenders, works at app-icon size | ≤8 letters, balanced | 9–12 letters | 13+ letters, or ambiguous letterforms (rn/m, l/I) | R |
| F3 | **Display-name limits**: WhatsApp Business, UPI merchant name, GST trade name, SMS sender ID (6 chars) | Fits | Needs a short form | No workable short form | R |
| F4 | **Export check**: works in US/EU/GCC/SEA if planned | Neutral there | Pronunciation drift | Negative meaning there | V |
| F5 | **Market test**: 5-second recall test with 10–20 target buyers, plus an A/B on ad CTR if possible | Highest recall in the set | Middle | Lowest recall, or misheard | V |
| F6 | **Founder & stakeholder fit**: emotional ownership. Numerology or vastu are noted if the founder cares, but **never** used to override A–E | Founders love it | Lukewarm | Veto | V |

## Scoring

- PASS = 2, WARN = 1, FAIL = 0.
- **N-A** (doesn't apply, for example F4 export for a city-only clinic) and **UNVERIFIED** (couldn't check live) are excluded from the denominator.
- **Score = earned / possible × 100**, always reported as `78 (6 unverified)`. Compute it with `scripts/score36.py`, never by hand.
- **Existing-use evidence counts:** if a search shows a same-category brand already trading under the name, D1 may be **FAIL** even without a registry search. Prior use is a trade mark risk in itself.
- Reading the score: ≥80 is strong, 65–79 is workable, <65 needs rework. Any D-FAIL is out.
- UNVERIFIED by default: D1 (IP India), D4 (MCA), E3 (Instagram), E5 (LinkedIn/WhatsApp), F5 (market test), F6 (founder fit). They become real only when the user supplies the result.
- **D1 and D4 are never PASS** without a live registry search. Treat them as UNVERIFIED and list them in next steps.
- Report the score **and** the three weakest points. A score alone hides the fatal flaw.
- Scores cluster, because many points are unverifiable. Treat a difference under 5 points as a tie, and rank by evidence (SKILL.md step 5).
